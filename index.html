Actor Line Highlighter

Tap an actor's name to highlight all of their lines. This page uses JavaScript, the DOM, and data-actor attributes to dynamically show and hide dialogue without reloading the page.

ALICE: I can't believe we're late again.

BOB: It wasn't my fault this time!

CHARLIE: Both of you relax - rehearsal hasn't even started.

ALICE: Good. Then we still have time to practice.

How This Page Works

This page highlights all lines for an actor when their name is clicked. Each name uses a data-actor tag, and JavaScript finds matching lines and highlights them while resetting the rest.
